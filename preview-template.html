What the reversal teaches

At ten basis points, validation IR was 1.06; later evaluation IR was −0.48. Retrospective attribution locates the main shortfall in equity underweights during 2023–2024, but does not prove a causal regime explanation. A reproducible failure to persist is useful evidence for research discipline—not a reason to retune the test until it succeeds.
